## Context
Today a two-player match ends the moment either player dies, so a single early mistake decides a race. Michael wants it to play like Mario Kart: a crash costs you time and points but you are put back on the track. The match is then about **points by the end of the last level**, not survival:
- A crash: the half halts briefly (the tumble), the poop is eased back to the middle of its screen, blinks for a few cycles, and play resumes with a clear screen (the next pair opens at mid-height, like after a fart). **Dying costs 2 × level points** (level 3: −6), **never below 0**.
- **Clearing a level earns 25 points.**
- The match ends when **both players have cleared the rectum** (the first one out waits under a "Made it out!" banner while the other keeps playing). **Higher points win; equal points go to the player with fewer crashes; still equal is a draw.**
- All of this is two-player only. Single player keeps one death ending the run, its scores and the leaderboard untouched.

Decisions from Michael (2026-11-08, AskUserQuestion): both finish the rectum; two-player only; the world halts during the respawn and restarts with a clear screen; fewer deaths breaks a tie, then draw.

Decisions made here, flagged so they can be overruled:
- The respawn is one delta-driven timeline (`respawnPose` in `src/versus.js`): crash tumble `crashMs` (700, the current `deathToOverMs`), glide to mid-screen `glideMs` (700), blink `blinkCycles` (3) × 2 × `blinkMs` (250) between alpha 1 and `blinkAlpha` (0.2). About 2.9 s out, then control returns with the usual 700 ms grace. No tweens drive the glide or blink, so the shared pause freezes it by not ticking.
- The crash keeps the death effects (dead face, shake, flash, haptics, fart sound, tumble) so it still feels like a crash; the poop's normal face comes back when the glide starts.
- The field is cleared when the glide starts: every pipe, item, bean and box that is not already fully behind the poop goes, and a finish line ahead goes too (it respawns when the level's pairs are done). Pairs cleared this way are spawned again from the shared course with the same pair numbers, so the player replays the same stretch and the two courses stay identical.
- Beans and a running probiotic survive a crash (Mario Kart drops your item, but the catch-up box already helps the trailing player; fewer rules). A fart cannot crash (unchanged).
- The **−N popup** uses the bacteria popup's colour; the **+25 popup** uses the mystery gold.
- The finished player's half shows `Made it out!` over `Waiting for P2…` (the level banner texts, no shade). A tap on that half lifts a shared pause; its keys and pad do nothing else.
- The result panel drops the OUT / Made it out! status line (both players always made it out now) and shows **💀 ×n** crashes under the pipes, so the tiebreak is visible. `versusOut` is removed.
- Version **0.7.0** (a rule change to a shipped mode), with its own What's new entry.
- Out of scope: a per-level or per-player respawn count limit, dropping beans on a crash, a "give up early" button, the How to play rows (README and What's new carry the rules; a versus How to play row is a separate ask).

## What the code says (verified 2026-11-08)
- `Play.state` is `'ready' | 'playing' | 'paused' | 'dead'` (`src/scenes/Play.js:168`). `die()` (:2077) guards on `'playing'` and on `effects.powerActive() || graceLeftMs > 0 || testInvincible()`, sets `slot.alive = false`, calls `endRun()` (state `'dead'`, stops every pipe / item / bean, in a match publishes the slot, sets `slot.ended`, reports `Versus.onRunEnded`), then the dead face, shake, flash, haptics, sound, tumble (an **unstored** angle tween over `deathToOverMs`) and `showOver()` (a no-op in a match). The `'dead'` block in `update()` (:2195) pins the tumbling poop at the floor.
- `win()` (:2064) is `endRun()` without the death effects: `slot.won`, gravity off, plain face, gold flash. `onFinishCrossed()` (:595) bumps `level` (not on the final level), plays the level sound, cancels a fart, then `win()` or `startLevelBreak()`: the one hook for a level bonus, and it must add the bonus **before** `win()` so the reported slot carries it.
- `Versus.onRunEnded(player)` (`src/scenes/Versus.js:190`) ends the match on the first report: status `'over'`, `haltRun()` on the other half, result after `deathToOverMs`. `haltRun()` (`Play.js:1982`) exists only for that. `showResult()` (:201) reads `matchResult(slots)` (score → pipes → survivor → draw, `src/versus.js:144`) and shows a status line from `slot.won` / `slot.alive` (`versusEscaped` / `versusOut`).

- The fart precedent: `endPower()` (:1648) stops the blink tween, alpha 1, gravity on, `graceLeftMs = T.beans.graceMs`, `centreNextGap = true; nextGapY = null; prefillPipes(); applyScroll()`; `spawnPipePair()` (:1335) numbers the pair `n = this.pipesSpawned`, opens at `centredGapY` when `centreNextGap`, and a centred pair never moves. `endLevelBreak()` (:649) does the same after `finishSpawned = false`. `prefillPipes()` (:1278) spawns nothing past `levelEnd(level)` and nothing while `finishSpawned`; the finish line spawns in `update()` when `pipesSpawned === levelEnd && !finishSpawned`.

Pipe scoring (:2217) credits a pair once `hasPassed(pipe.x, T.pipeWidth, poop.x)` (fully behind), so "every object whose right edge is not yet behind the poop's left edge" is exactly the uncredited set.

## Design

**Knobs** (`src/config.js`, `TUNING.versus`): `scoring: { deathPenaltyPerLevel: 2, levelClearBonus: 25 }`, `respawn: { crashMs: 700, glideMs: 700, blinkMs: 250, blinkCycles: 3, blinkAlpha: 0.2 }`, each with a unit comment.

**Pure logic.**
- `src/logic.js`: `deathPenalty(level, t)` = `deathPenaltyPerLevel × level`; `scoreAfterDeath(score, level, t)` = `max(0, score − penalty)` (next to `scoreAfterItem`). `scorePopup(kind, t, mult, pairs, level = 1)` gains `'death'` → `−N` in `popup.bacteriaColor` and `'levelClear'` → `+25` in `mystery.color`. `chromeIntent` / `padIntent`: a new state `'respawn'` behaves like `'playing'` for pause / esc / a pad nav press (so ⏸, P, Esc and the d-pad still pause the match mid-respawn) and ignores tap / flap / power; the JSDoc unions and the two STATES test arrays gain it.
- `src/versus.js`: `slotTemplate()` drops `alive`, adds `deaths: 0`, `respawning: false`, `alpha: 1`. `respawnTotalMs(t)`; `respawnPose(elapsedMs, from, t)` → `{ phase: 'crash' | 'glide' | 'blink' | 'done', y, angle, alpha }`: crash = `{ y: from.y, angle: from.angle, alpha: 1 }` (physics owns the tumble; `from` is null until the glide starts); glide eases (`1 − (1 − r)²`, the fart's ease) from `from` to `(GAME_HEIGHT / 2, 0)`; blink holds the centre with a triangle wave 1 → `blinkAlpha` → 1 per cycle; done = `{ y: centre, angle: 0, alpha: 1 }`. `ghostPlacement` multiplies by `other.alpha ?? 1` and zeroes only when `other.ended` (so the rival's blink shows on the ghost). `matchResult(slots)` → score, then **fewer `deaths`** (`by: 'deaths'`), else draw; `pipesPassed` and the survivor rule go.

**Play half, the respawn** (`src/scenes/Play.js`; all of it inside `if (this.match)`, single player unchanged):
- `die()`: after the guards, `if (this.match) this.startRespawn(); else this.endRun();` then the shared death effects (store the tumble tween as `this.deathTween`), then `if (!this.match) this.showOver();`. No `slot.alive`.
- `startRespawn()`: `state = 'respawn'`, `respawnElapsed = 0`, `respawnFrom = null`, `endSweep()`, every pipe / item / bean velocity 0 (the world halts; the background stops because `state !== 'playing'`), `score = scoreAfterDeath(score, level, T)`, `scoreText`, `showScorePopup('death', 1, 0, level)`, `refreshProbioticHud()`, `slot.deaths += 1`, `slot.respawning = true`, `publishSlot()`.
- `tickRespawn(delta)` (from `update()` while `state === 'respawn'`): `respawnElapsed += delta`; `pose = respawnPose(elapsed, respawnFrom, T)`. The first frame in `'glide'`: `deathTween.stop()`, gravity off, velocity 0, `respawnFrom = { y: poop.y, angle: poop.angle }` (re-read the pose), `clearFieldAhead()`, `refreshPoopLook()`. In glide / blink: `poop.y = pose.y; setAngle(pose.angle); setAlpha(pose.alpha)`. In `'done'`: `endRespawn()`. The floor pin in `update()` runs for `'dead'` and the crash phase too.
- `clearFieldAhead()`: destroy every pipe / item / bean that is not yet credited, with the scoring predicate itself: `!hasPassed(obj.x, obj.displayWidth, poop.x)` (sprite x is the centre; `hasPassed` is `x + w / 2 < poop.x`), iterating a copy of each group as `skipToFinish` does; the mystery reveal is a fixed HUD container, left alone. Destroy `finishLine` when it is ahead and set `finishSpawned = false`; `pipesSpawned = pipesPassed`; `nextGapY = null`; `endSweep()`. Credited pairs stay and scroll off. Replayed pairs carry the **same pair numbers** from the shared course (gap, box spots, moving pairs), not necessarily identical items: `course.item` / `course.bean` read score and bag state, and a box collected before the crash comes back.
- `endRespawn()`: `setAlpha(1)`, `state = 'playing'`, gravity on, `setVelocityY(0)`, `graceLeftMs = T.beans.graceMs`, `centreNextGap = true; nextGapY = null; prefillPipes(); applyScroll()`, `slot.respawning = false`, `refreshPoopLook()`, `publishSlot()`.
- Pause interplay: `pausePlay()` accepts `'respawn'` as well and remembers `respawnPaused = (state === 'respawn')`; `resumePlay()`, when `respawnPaused`, returns the half to `'respawn'` (physics, effects, bob, banner) **without** the bounce or the flap sound and returns. `pauseEffects` / `resumeEffects` add `deathTween` (nulled after every `stop()`, the `breakGlide` pattern). `autoPause()` accepts `'respawn'`. `Versus.setPaused(true)` pauses halves in `'playing'` or `'respawn'`; the resume branch is unchanged (`'paused' && !levelBreak`). `Versus.anyLive()` → a half is `'playing'` or `'respawn'`.
- **Chrome state comes from the match, not the half** (review finding: a P1 that is respawning or has finished would otherwise open Settings / About / … with `pause: false` and P2 would fly on under the overlay, and a pad nav press would not pause). `chromeArgs()` in a match passes `state` = `'paused'` when this half is in its own level break or the shared pause is on, else `'playing'` when `anyLive()`, else `this.state`. `applyChrome`'s overlay cases pause when `result.pause || (this.match && host.anyLive())` (so a half in its own level break opening Settings pauses the other half too, which today it does not). `padIntent` then gives `pauseAndFocus` on a finished or respawning P1. The derived `'paused'` honours `resumeLocked` as before.
- `flap()`: the `'dead'` early return applies to single player only; in a match, after the tray check, a `'dead'` or `'respawn'` half goes to `handleChrome('tap')`, which with the derived state resumes a shared pause and is otherwise inert (`doFlap` guards on `'playing'`). So a tablet P1 that finished first can still reach ⏸, mute and the overlays.
- `startRespawn()` also `setCharged(false)` (the stink timer's `refreshPoopLook` would otherwise overwrite the dead face during the crash); `endRespawn()` restores `setCharged(true)` when `effects.powerReady()` (the `endPower` pattern). Beans stay in `effects`.
- The match tumble runs over `respawn.crashMs` (single player keeps `deathToOverMs`), so the two knobs cannot drift apart silently.
- The glide's angle target is the nearest multiple of 360 to the crash angle (the tumble ends near 180°; easing to 0 would unspin it backwards).
- `applyLocale()` re-shows the escaped banner when `this.won && this.match` (its `refreshLevelBanner()` would otherwise overwrite it with "LEVEL n").
- `publishSlot()` adds `slot.alpha = poop.alpha`. `refreshGhost()`: `out = other.ended` only.
- `haltRun()` is removed (nothing calls it once the match no longer ends on the first report).

**Scoring and the end** (`Play.js` + `src/scenes/Versus.js`):
- `onFinishCrossed()`: in a match, before `win()` / `startLevelBreak()`: `score += T.versus.scoring.levelClearBonus`, `scoreText`, `refreshProbioticHud()`; the `+25` popup is shown **after** `startLevelBreak()` returns (a popup started before it would be frozen at alpha 0 by `pauseEffects` and play under the next level's first pair; one started while paused is not paused and shows dimmed under the shade) and at once on the final level.
- `win()` in a match: after `endRun()`, `showEscapedBanner()`: `levelTitle` = `versusEscaped`, `levelHint` = `versusWaiting` with the other player's number, both visible, no shade (they stay until the result panel covers them; nothing else touches them on a `'dead'` half).
- `Versus.onRunEnded(player)`: ignore unless `status === 'playing'`; return unless **both** slots are `ended`; then status `'over'`, clear the shared pause, result after `deathToOverMs`. No halt.
- `showResult()`: per slot, under the pipes row, `💀 ×${slot.deaths}` (no string needed); the status line goes. Headline and buttons unchanged.
- `src/i18n.js`: `versusWaiting: 'Waiting for P{n}…'` / `'等待玩家{n}…'`; remove `versusOut`. `TRANSLATION_NOTES.md` rows and the Two players rule follow.

## Risks and notes
- `'respawn'` is a fifth Play state: every `state === 'playing'` gate in `update()` (scroll, spawn, scoring, bobbing, tilt) stays closed during it by construction; the gates that must **open** (pause inputs, `pausePlay`, `autoPause`, `Versus.setPaused`) are listed above. The step-2 smoke drives a pause mid-blink to prove the elapsed clock and the alpha freeze.
- Clearing by "right edge behind the poop" leaves a pair the poop crashed into gone and already-credited pairs in place; `pipesSpawned = pipesPassed` keeps the level's progress, the finish-line trigger and the course's pair numbers aligned. The smoke asserts `pipesSpawned === pipesPassed`, no object ahead, and the first new pair centred.
- A crash while a finish line is on screen but not crossed: the line is destroyed and `finishSpawned = false`; with `pipesSpawned === levelEnd`, `prefillPipes()` spawns nothing and `update()` spawns the line again after one spacing. Covered by a smoke check.
- `respawnPose` is pure in elapsed time, so a shared pause only has to stop the clock; the one tween (the tumble) is in `pauseEffects`.
- The finished half sits in `'dead'` for as long as the other plays; `Versus.setPaused` never touches it, its tray stays P1's as before. If P1 finishes first its tray still drives Settings etc. for both (unchanged).
- The match-derived chrome state is a behaviour change for an existing case too: a half in its own level break that opens Settings now pauses the other half (today it flies on under the overlay). Flagged, not optional: the overlay covers both halves.
- Review (Plan agent, 2026-11-08) folded in: overlay / pad pause from a waiting or respawning P1, the tray on a finished half, `setCharged` during the crash, the `+25` popup timing, `deathTween` nulling and `crashMs`, the escaped banner on a locale change, the `hasPassed` clearing predicate, the glide's angle target, the smoke's atomic re-pin and the two other kills.
- Phone / pad feel of the 2.9 s respawn and the blink cannot be verified here; the timings are knobs.
